# data

Place dataset(s) in this folder.

Add data dictionary to this file.

## Dataset 1: `super-bowl-dataset-1.csv`

- `Game`: Number assigned to each Super Bowl game.  
- `Date`: Date on which the Super Bowl game took place.  
- `Winning.team`: Name of the team that won the game.  
- `Winning.Team.Points`: Points scored by the winning team.  
- `Winning.Team.Conference`: Conference of the winning team.
- `Score`: Final score showing points for both teams.  
- `Losing.team`: Name of the team that lost the game.
- `Losing.Team.Points`: Points scored by the losing team.
- `Losing.Team.Conference`: Conference of the losing team. 
- `Venue`: Location where the game was held.
- `City`: City where the game was held.
- `Attendance`: Number of people attending the game.
- `Network`: Television network that broadcasted the game.
- `Average.U.S..Viewers`: Average number of viewers in the U.S. who watched the game.
- `Rating`: TV rating showing percentage of households with TVs tuned in.
- `Share`: Share of viewers tuned in among households watching TV.
- `Cost.Per.30s.Ad`: Cost of a 30-second advertisement during the game.
- `Notes`: Additional details or interesting facts about the game.

## Dataset 2: `super-bowl-dataset-2.csv`

- `Date`: The date which the Super Bowl was played in month, day, and year.
- `SB`: The Super Bowl number in Roman numeral and in numeric form to represent the specific championship game.
- `Winner`: The team that won the Super Bow shown by the categorical variable of the NFL team names.
- `Winner.Pts`: The number of points scored by the winning team; a numerical discrete variable.
- `Loser`: The team that lost the Super Bowl shown by the categorical variable the of NFL team names.
- `Loser.Pts`: The number of points scored by the losing team; a numerical discrete variable.
- `MVP`: The player awarded Most Valuable Player for a Super Bowl game shown by the categorical variable of the player names.
- `Stadium`: The name of the stadium where the Super Bowl was played shown as a categorical variable.
- `City`:  The city in which the stadium is located shown as a categorical variable.
- `State`: The U.S. state where the Super Bowl was played shown as a categorical variable.
